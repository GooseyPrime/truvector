# What this site may say — read before adding content

## The rule

**Describe the mechanism, never the stage.** What the checkpoint does, and how,
is the subject of this site. How far along any of it is, is not — not in a
table, not in a badge, not in a sentence, not in a caption.

Two requirements sit behind that, and they are easy to mistake for each other:

1. **Never describe something as working that does not work.** A reader who
   finds one overstatement stops believing the rest of the page, and nothing
   written afterwards repairs it.
2. **Never publish how far along anything is.** No roadmap, no phases, no launch
   dates, no "coming soon", no "not yet built", no revenue or funding state, no
   headcount. Status belongs only in a document sent directly to a funding body
   or an investor — never on a public page.

A stage label satisfies the first and breaks the second. Mechanism satisfies
both: it is true, checkable, and says nothing about a calendar.

The funding body is never named on this site, and neither is the programme it
runs.

---

## How to write about something that is not finished

The honest move is not a status label. It is to change what kind of sentence
you are writing.

| Instead of a stage | Write the mechanism, or the open question |
|---|---|
| "Quorum — specified, not built" | Leave it out. A named feature with no mechanism to describe has nothing to say to a reader yet. |
| "Multi-model triangulation — future stage" | Leave it out, or, if the idea matters, state the question: what would it take for two systems to disagree in a way that is informative rather than noisy? |
| "Kinematic validation — under test" | State it as a research direction and give the observation that would disprove it. That is a stronger sentence than any status, and it is the form the science pages already use. |
| "Calibrated" | Say what is measured and what is not. "Detected and measured" is a fact; "calibrated" is a term of art that promises an error bar, so do not use it without one. |

A research direction published with its own falsification test is not a
weakness. It is the thing a technical reader is looking for, and it cannot be
misread as a shipping promise.

## Describable in the present tense

These are mechanism, and may be written plainly as what the system does:

- The three questions asked before an action: where the retrieved material came
  from, whether the sources are really separate, and whether they agree.
- The Allow · Review · Block outcome, and what each one means for the caller.
- The result object, including supporting and conflicting sources kept as
  separate fields rather than collapsed into one score.
- Evaluating a statement against a stable corpus.
- Detecting contradiction and divergence — as detected and measured, and not as
  calibrated.
- Discounting support that shares an origin, with the discount exposed rather
  than folded into a single confidence number.
- The decision path, logged so it can be reconstructed afterwards.

---

## Never publishable

No wording makes any of these acceptable:

- Throughput, capacity, latency, or cost-per-call figures.
- Capital requirements, valuations, revenue, or any figure in currency.
- Named pilots, named customers, named partners, or named institutions —
  including a named institution nearby, which reads as an affiliation whether or
  not one is asserted.
- A benchmark number without the protocol published alongside it. A number
  nobody can reproduce is worth less than no number.
- Any statement that a capability exists when it does not, in any tense or
  hedge.
- Internal business detail: what has or has not shipped, sold, been filed, been
  declined, or been staffed.

---

## Vocabulary

- **Statement** — a single assertion being checked.
- **Story** — something spreading across many sources.
- **Retrieved material** / **retrieved sources** — what a system looked up
  before acting.
- **Result object** — what the checkpoint returns.

The words *claim*, *evidence*, *evidentiary*, *assertion*, *witness*,
*arbitration*, *lineage* and *sufficiency* are not used. They come from a
courtroom, and the reader spends their attention decoding the metaphor instead
of understanding the product.

---

## Keeping the sites in step

If what the system does changes, update this file, the affected page copy, and
the InTellMe parent site in the same change. A capability described one way on
one site and another way on the other is a discrepancy a reader will find, and
they will trust neither page afterwards.
